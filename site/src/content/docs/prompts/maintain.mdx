title: Change & maintain

import Prompt from '../../../components/Prompt.astro'

Every change goes through `video.json` or `scenario.ts`, never through the generated `video/`
folder. So these prompts are safe to run again and again.

## After the app changes

<Prompt
    title="Re-record after a UI change"
    text={`The customers table got new columns. Re-record <videosDir>/customers-search with the
existing scenario, re-render with the current video.json, and show me frames at each callout.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Check every video after a release"
    text={`We just shipped <release / redesign>. Run \`reelson verify --all\`. For every demo that no longer runs
or no longer fits (a missing marker, a zoom or trim pointing at a different click), fix scenario.ts
or video.json, keep the new take with \`reelson verify <slug> --update\`, then \`reelson render --all\`.
Summarise what changed per video.`}
/>

## Wording, timing and look

<Prompt
    title="Tweak a video without re-recording"
    text={`In <videosDir>/customers-search, change callout 2 to "Search by name or email", make the zoom
tighter (scale 2.0) and 1 s shorter, then re-render.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Compare another intro or outro"
    text={`Render <videosDir>/customers-search with the minimal intro, no recap and the endcard
outro, next to the current one so I can compare.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Let me edit it myself"
    text={`Start \`reelson studio <slug> --no-open\` in the background and give me the URL. While it runs, re-read
video.json before any change you make, because my edits in the studio are saved there.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Make the cursor fit a slow page"
    text={`In <slug>, the dragged card trails the cursor. Measure the gap on the drag from the recording and
set "cursor": { "lag": <seconds> } in video.json, then show me snapshots during the drag.`}
/>

## Formats and sound

<Prompt
    title="A GIF for the README"
    text={`Render <videosDir>/customers-search also as a GIF for the README, and add it to the README
under <section> with a link to the MP4.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Phone and square versions"
    text={`Record the phone and square takes for <slug> (reelson record <slug> --mobile, then --square),
keeping the marker labels, then render --all-formats. Add "formats": ["portrait", "square"] to its
video.json so every future render makes them too.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Add a voice-over"
    text={`Turn on the voice-over for <slug> with <openai / elevenlabs / piper>. Give terse callouts a
full-sentence "say", add a short intro line, render, and tell me if any line runs past its step.`}
/>

## Publish

<Prompt
    title="Publish a video"
    note="Uploads are public actions: the agent does a dry run and waits for your go-ahead."
    text={`Publish <slug> to the <youtube> channel. Run \`reelson publish <slug> --to <youtube> --dry-run\` first
and show me the title and description; only upload after I say so.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Replace a published video after a re-record"
    text={`<slug> was re-recorded. Run \`reelson publish <slug> --to <youtube> --replace --dry-run\`, show me the
plan, and after I confirm, replace it. Then tell me the new video id so I can update our link.`}
/>

<Prompt
    title="Fix the words of a published video"
    text={`Change the YouTube title of <slug> to "<New title>" and its description to mention <…>. Put the words
in video.json "publish", then \`reelson publish <slug> --to <youtube> --update\` (same URL, video untouched).`}
/>
