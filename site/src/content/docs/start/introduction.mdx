reelson turns a prompt into a finished, branded demo video of a web app. It is made of two
[Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io) and a `reelson` CLI. It works with Claude Code, Codex
and any agent that reads `SKILL.md` skills.

<CardGrid>
    <Card title="reelson-record" icon="desktop">
        Runs a Playwright walkthrough of your app with a visible, human-paced cursor. It hides
        dev chrome and logs step markers and clicks.
        **Output:** `recording.mp4` + `markers.json`
    </Card>
    <Card title="reelson-compose" icon="puzzle">
        Builds a [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com) composition around the footage:
        poster intro, framed recording, callouts, cursor-timed zooms, recap, brand outro and music.
        **Output:** `video/renders/<slug>.mp4`
    </Card>
</CardGrid>

## What you get

- **A real browser, a believable cursor.** The page is filmed with Chrome's screencast
  (every painted frame at 2x pixels) and assembled into a smooth 30 fps clip. The cursor glides
  on curved, eased paths and lands slightly off-centre. Typing has uneven delays. The pacing
  is seeded, so every re-record is identical.
- **Callouts and zooms that time themselves.** A `demo.marker()` in the scenario becomes a
  numbered callout and a row in the recap. A zoom is anchored to click numbers, so it rides in
  with the cursor and finishes before the click.
- **Your brand everywhere.** Name, colours, logo, language (built-in strings for 18 languages)
  and one music bed per project, all set in `reelson.config.json`.
- **Nothing to redo after a UI change.** `reelson verify --all` re-records every demo and
  reports anything that no longer fits.

## What lives in your repo

<FileTree>
- reelson.config.json  brand, language, music, recording defaults
- docs/videos/
  - customers-search/
    - scenario.ts  what happens (you or the agent write it)
    - markers.json  when it happened (generated, committed)
    - video.json  how it looks: callouts, zooms, trim
    - published.json  where it was uploaded (after `reelson publish`)
    - recording.mp4  generated, git-ignored
    - video/  generated HyperFrames project and renders/, git-ignored
</FileTree>

## Requirements

- **Node 22.18+** and **ffmpeg** (`brew install ffmpeg`)
- The app you record, running locally
- HyperFrames is fetched by `npx` on first use, at a pinned version
